This file is a trimmed derivative of the OpenJLPT kanji dataset
(https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT), CC BY-SA 4.0.

Level assignments (N5, N4, N3, N2, N1) come from Jonathan Waller's JLPT
Resources (https://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/), CC BY. They are unofficial.
The Japan Foundation has not published official kanji lists for the
five-level JLPT used since 2010. KANJIDIC2's older four-level jlpt field
(1–4, from before 2010) is not used here.

On readings, kun readings, and English meanings come from KANJIDIC2,
the property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group
(EDRDG), used under CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project
https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html

Each entry keeps only the level, meanings, on readings, and kun readings.
Retrieved 12 October 2026 from OpenJLPT data/json/kanji/n5.json through
n1.json. Rebuild with: node tools/build-jlpt-kanji.js

Adding a lesson does not require rebuilding this file. The Kanji page
reads lessons/index.json and the lesson files when it opens. A character
that is not in this file is still listed, under "No JLPT level", without
a dictionary gloss.
